# Pourquoi ? — Branding Canon

Status: P7 candidate / human gate required

## Brand core
- Display name: `Pourquoi ?`
- Promise: `1 question • 1 réponse`
- Audience: children roughly 6–9 years old
- Mascot: recurring brown-haired boy in a red hoodie
- Visual language: bright blue sky, white clouds, colorful question bubbles, yellow sparkles, clean 2D/3D hybrid educational style

## Logo
- Word `Pourquoi` in saturated blue with a white outline
- Question mark in red with a white outline
- Strong rounded display type, readable at small size
- Never cover the mascot's face with branding or CTA layers

## Motion identity
### Intro bumper
- Duration target: 2.0–2.5 s
- Current candidate: 2.200 s
- Structure: question bubbles pop → logo lands → tagline appears → immediate cut into episode hook
- The episode hook itself is not replaced by the bumper

### Outro / end screen
- Duration target: 5–8 s
- Current candidate: 7.000 s
- Two clean video recommendation slots
- One subscribe/avatar circle
- CTA stays below the mascot's face
- Same visual and sonic motif as the intro

## Sonic identity
- One short four-note motif reused in intro and outro
- Current motif contour: rising 4-note educational sparkle
- Bright pluck timbre, no spoken brand tag required
- Same motif at both ends for memory consistency

## Reuse rule
Every episode may reuse the exact same P7 intro/outro/jingle unless a later branding version is explicitly human-approved.

## P7 gate
- Intro ≤ 2.5 s
- Outro 5–8 s
- Same sonic motif intro/outro
- No CTA over face
- 1920×1080 / 24 fps master
- Human gate before merging P7 into the production chain
